ArduPilot · Jetson · USDA NAIP 0.6 m

Image-based navigation against cached NAIP imagery

A ground planner caches NAIP along a route and exports a mission bundle. On the aircraft, a Jetson matches the downward camera to that imagery and feeds ArduPilot a position through MAVLink GPS_INPUT, so the autopilot flies the mission without satellites.

Hardware and links

wired data or power MAVLink serial radio link before flight, USB or Wi-Fi

What happens to each frame

Bench result on a synthetic stand-in for NAIP (the build sandbox could not reach NAIP servers): 97 to 100% of frames matched, 2.1 to 2.6 m median error, 4 to 6 m at the 95th percentile, about 50 ms per frame on a laptop CPU. Real imagery still needs testing.

ArduPilot settings

  • SERIAL2_PROTOCOL 2, SERIAL2_BAUD 921
  • GPS1_TYPE 14 so the Jetson is GPS1 (named GPS_TYPE before 4.6)
  • EK3_SRC1_POSXY 3, VELXY 3, POSZ 1 (baro), YAW 1 (compass)
  • GPS_AUTO_SWITCH 0 so the real GNSS on GPS2 never takes over
  • FS_EKF_ACTION 1: land if the position estimate degrades

Full list in docs/ardupilot_visnav.parm. Check each name against your firmware and try it in SITL first.

Operating limits

  • Camera footprint needs about 250 map pixels (150 m). A 90° lens at 100 to 120 m AGL is enough.
  • Flat-ground assumption; hilly sites need the lidar or a DEM.
  • NAIP is summer imagery 2 to 3 years old. Snow, harvest and new building reduce matches.
  • Water, dense canopy and bare uniform fields give no fixes. Plan routes over textured ground.
  • NAIP covers the contiguous US only.
